## ⏱️ Second-by-Second Storyboard & Voiceover (English)

### Scene 1: The Hook & The Problem (0:00 – 0:18)
- **On Screen**: Split screen or quick montage: A citizen staring at 10 open browser tabs (`oesterreich.gv.at`, `bmi.gv.at`, `migration.gv.at`, PDF forms). Overlay text: *"Public Administration in 2026"*.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Austria has world-class public services. But for citizens, navigating them means getting lost in dozens of fragmented portals, complex legalese, and confusing PDF forms. Generic AI tools can't solve this—they hallucinate deadlines and invent legal requirements."*

---

### Scene 2: Introducing Kompetent (0:18 – 0:32)
- **On Screen**: Smooth zoom-in to the clean, official UI of **Kompetent** at `competent.shiv.ovh`. Austrian crest, `kp-navy` accents, and the title: *"Digitaler Bürgerassistent 2030"*.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Meet **Kompetent**—Austria’s universal, legally grounded AI citizen assistant for 2030. Built by Goi Garg, Cajetan Perwein, Jeet Gupta, and Reyan Zein, it understands colloquial everyday language, protects citizen privacy, and turns bureaucratic friction into empathetic guidance."*

---

### Scene 3: Live Citizen Interaction & Zero Hallucination (0:32 – 0:58)
- **On Screen**: Mouse cursor types a real-world, multi-part prompt into the chat box:
  > `"Hi! I just relocated to Vienna for a software engineering job. What do I need to register my address and what permit do I need?"`
- **Actions Recorded**:
  1. Instant streaming response appears with the badge **"KI-Antwort"**.
  2. The response explains the 3-day statutory deadline in plain language, keeping the official term (*Meldezettel*) in brackets.
  3. Camera highlights the green **"Geprüfte Angabe"** badge.
  4. Cursor clicks the verified citation source: instantly opens the official live BMI application form PDF (*Antragsformular B*).
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Kompetent doesn't just chat. It breaks down complex procedures into plain words, cites only verified Austrian government sources, and provides direct, live application forms. Frustration and guesswork are completely eliminated."*

---

### Scene 4: Universal Inclusivity & Accessibility (0:58 – 1:12)
- **On Screen**: Quick demonstration of accessibility features:
  - Toggle to **Leichte Sprache (Simple Language)** or **High-Contrast / Large Text Mode**.
  - Show seamless multilingual answering (English, German, Arabic RTL layout).
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Designed for everyone: from seniors needing 200% text zoom and Simple Language, to international specialists communicating in English or Arabic. Fully compliant with WCAG 2.2 AA."*

---

### Scene 5: Seamless Human Caseworker Handover (1:12 – 1:32)
- **On Screen**:
  1. The user clicks the prominent button: **"Mit einer Person sprechen" (Speak with a person)**.
  2. The assistant displays: *"Connecting you with a caseworker..."*
  3. **Switch to second browser tab**: Open `competent.shiv.ovh/portal`.
  4. The caseworker dashboard highlights the new incoming case.
  5. Caseworker clicks **"Fall ansehen"**—an automated, structured **Case Dossier** in German is displayed with extracted facts, urgency deadlines, and missing documents.
  6. Caseworker clicks **"Übernehmen" (Take Over)**. Citizen chat instantly shows: *"A caseworker has joined the conversation."*
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"When cases get complex, Kompetent never leaves citizens stranded. With one click, the citizen requests a human officer. Behind the scenes, the AI compiles a structured Case Dossier—saving civil servants up to 10 minutes of manual data entry per inquiry, with zero media disruption."*

---

### Scene 6: Vision 2030 & Next Steps (1:32 – 1:45)
- **On Screen**: Quick showcase of the Roadmap Slide from `docs/presentation.html`:
  - Icons for: WhatsApp/Telegram API, Automated Form Filling & Direct ZMR/BMI Submission, and ID Austria Qualified Signature.
  - Final card showing: `competent.shiv.ovh` | Team: Goi Garg · Cajetan Perwein · Jeet Gupta · Reyan Zein.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Our 2030 roadmap takes this even further: cross-platform access via WhatsApp, automated direct form submission to ministry registries, and ID Austria signatures.*  
  > *This is public service for the next decade. Experience Kompetent live at **competent.shiv.ovh**."*

## 🛠️ Recording Checklist for the Team

1. **Browser Preparation**:
   - Open Chrome or Arc at 1920×1080 resolution (100% zoom).
   - Tab 1: `https://competent.shiv.ovh` (Citizen interface)
   - Tab 2: `https://competent.shiv.ovh/portal` (Caseworker portal, logged in)
   - Tab 3: `https://competent.shiv.ovh/presentation` (Pitch deck roadmap slide)
2. **Audio Setup**:
   - Use a clear USB microphone (avoid laptop echo).
   - Speak in an energetic, confident, and professional tone.
3. **Pacing**:
   - Rehearse once with a stopwatch; aim for **1:35 to 1:45**.
   - Keep cursor movements smooth and deliberate (avoid frantic mouse jiggling).
4. **Export**:
   - Upload MP4 to YouTube (Unlisted or Public) or Loom.
   - Add the video link directly to `readme.md`.
